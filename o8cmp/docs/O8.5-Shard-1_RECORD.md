## What "module sharding" delivered (O8.5-Shard-1)

The single-scope IIFE was split into **three scope-isolated shards** joined by a **tiny explicit contract** — a `_0xmod` object whose only members are `log` and `mc`:

| shard | contents | contract access |
|---|---|---|
| 0 — foundation | console gate, LOG_LEVEL, Log module, log-lock (`GoogleUnlock` + digest), suite head (SUITE_VERSION/INSTANCE_ID/banner/config diag), MemberCount | exports `log`, `mc` |
| 1 — engine | run-key guard, controller/signal, disposables, release/scuttle, chunk capture, decode region, quest runners, orchestrator, boot dance | imports `Log`, `MemberCount` (aliases at shard top); nothing else |
| 2 — decoy | generator noise block (unchanged, standalone) | none |

Scope isolation verified behaviorally: engine references *only* `Log`/`MemberCount` from the contract; every other identifier lives inside its own shard. This is the seam design that makes **per-shard obfuscation with different settings** possible in Shard-2 (each shard = independent naming universe/string array once wrapped by obfuscator.io).
