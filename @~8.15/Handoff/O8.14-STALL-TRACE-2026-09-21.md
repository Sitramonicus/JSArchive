during the stall and asserts it comes out, while a `[Google Catalog]`-tagged line is held back.

That is the bug the review caught before shipping; the fix composes both tags from char codes, so the
allow-list itself plants no vocabulary (leak census flagged the literal version; re-run: no `host` hit).
