# `GoogleUblock is not defined` after a console F5 — defect report and fix (2026-09-21)

**Build in question:** 17 (`42b84dc8`). **Fixed in:** 18 (`b5773b53`).
**Operator report (trace #3):** four passes in the console. Pass 0 (main client) and Pass 3 (a freshly
restarted client) worked — `GoogleUblock('ripcord')` answered with `[Google diag] Host config`, the
Store/Pocket checks, `true`, `phase-t`, `GoogleSnag` at +14.3 s. Passes 1, 2 and 4 — all after a
**console F5 on the same page** — ran to `Uncaught ReferenceError: GoogleUblock is not defined`, and
each of them also showed the garden teardown.

---

## 1. What a console "F5" actually is (why the passes differ)

A console clear is **not** a page reload. `window`, its globals, its timers and its listeners all
survive. So passes 1/2/4 were a *second paste into a dirty page*, while pass 3 was a clean page. That
single difference is the whole defect: the first paste leaves residue that decides what the second
paste is allowed to do.

## 2. Root cause — two coupled defects

### 2.1 Every benign stand-down took the claim surface away

Shard-a publishes the entry point (`window.GoogleUblock`) and treats it as belonging to **the chain**.
The chain stops itself for many benign reasons, and each of those sites called the hard deleter
`_0xmod.v814дебдмл166.v814mosஇsu65()`:

| piece | site | stand-down reason |
|---|---|---|
| e1 | 293 | profile pinned below the needed level |
| e1 | 322 | nothing queued for this pass |
| e1 | 400 | pockets incomplete |
| e1 | 419 | capability check refused |
| e2 | 37 | no eligible chores |
| e4 | 137 | latency shape implausible (instrumented/debug-stepped clock) |
| e4 | 295 | chore chain finished |
| e4 | 297 | chain had no work |
| e4 | 306 | boot check never saw a venue ("take the claim surface away") |
| e4 | 309 | boot error |

So on a page where the machinery had merely found nothing to do, the console was left with **no claim
surface at all** — and the `[Host 8.14]` banner and the garden fallback kept printing afterwards, so
the page looked alive while there was nothing left to call. That is exactly the operator's "the script
won't put out `GoogleUblock` at all".

### 2.2 The exclusion was by name, not by ownership — the "residual thing"

The once-only guards and the deleter keyed on *the name* `GoogleUblock` and on globals the previous
paste had left behind (`lexProbeA`, `lexSetPins`, `lexProbeX`, `lexProbeU`, `lexVerifyL`, …). An older
session therefore (a) could delete an entry point it did not own, and (b) then had no path that would
ever publish one again — the residue *prevented the creation of a new one* and **persisted across the
console F5**, which is the operator's stated concern. Nothing superseded anything: the old session
simply outlived the new paste as a blocker.

## 3. The fix (build 18)

1. **Benign stand-downs keep the claim surface.** The ten sites above now call
   `_0xmod._standDown('<reason>')`, which records why the chain stopped and leaves
   `window.GoogleUblock` alone. The entry point's lifetime is the **window's**, not the chain's.
   Hard removals stay, and only three exist: the release verb (`close()`), the window expiring
   unclaimed, and the environment bails (`_0xenvOk` / `_0xharness`) — none of which is reachable on a
   page where an entry point was ever published.
2. **Ownership.** Shard-a records the function it published (`_0xmod._entry = _0xgu`). The
   window-expiry fallback returns **silently** when `window.GoogleUblock !== _0xmod._entry`: a
   superseded session neither deletes a newer entry point nor paints its garden over a live one. The
   hard deleter
   (`_0xmod.v814дебдмл166.v814mosஇsu65()`) is gated on the same check, so it can only ever remove the
   session's own function.
3. **A wrong or late passphrase is not a decoder tell.** Nothing in the fix keys on *how* a claim
   arrived or *when*: an in-window call, a pre-set `名`, a late call and a call after a console clear
   are all the same shape to the script. What still distinguishes a decoder from a user is unchanged —
   an unclaimed session stays silent, does no venue work, and stands its own machinery down.

## 5. What did NOT change

* **Anti-decoder behaviour:** an unclaimed session is still silent (Host + MemberCount + Player
  welcome only), still makes no venue calls, and still tears its machinery down at the window's end.
  Input-spam decoders get nothing from the entry point; the entry point's *presence* is the documented
  contract, not a tell.
* The carrier format, the loader, the decoy/honey/tube faces and `rotation.json` are unchanged
  (`55895e58` / `88397e56` / `ed0db1cd` / `5bc7746c` are byte-identical to build 17). The **cover**
  changed (`28da49e1` → `6239c8e0`) because the payload it carries grew by 17.9 KB; the embed process,
  occupancy discipline and grain are the same.
* Cost: payload **+17.9 KB** (2 295 382 → 2 313 305 B).
